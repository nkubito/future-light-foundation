Donation transparency

We want every supporter to understand how this donation flow works.

  • This is a manual transfer process, not an automatic online checkout.
  • The website does not initiate or confirm payments.
  • A donation is not verified until the foundation checks the transaction reference.
  • Always confirm the recipient name and details shown by your provider before sending money.
Only send money if the recipient details displayed by your Mobile Money provider match the intended foundation recipient. If anything looks wrong, stop and contact the foundation first.
