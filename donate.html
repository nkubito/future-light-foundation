How to complete your Mobile Money donation

  1. Open Mobile Money

    Use your Mobile Money menu on your phone.

  2. Enter the number and amount

    Use 0792157588 and the amount you selected. Carefully check the recipient name and details shown by your provider before confirming.

  3. Keep your transaction reference

    Save the confirmation message or transaction ID for your records.

  4. Share the reference with us

    Email the foundation with your donation amount and transaction reference so the team can verify the transfer.

Clear, careful giving

We want every supporter to know exactly what happens when they donate.

  • Mobile Money is the current available payment method.
  • Debit and credit card payments are not available yet; we will add them only after a secure payment provider is set up.
  • Bank transfer supporters should contact the foundation before sending funds.
  • The website does not collect card details or automatically process payments.
  • Donations are only verified after the foundation checks the transaction reference.
Before sending money, confirm that the recipient details shown by your Mobile Money provider match the intended foundation recipient. If anything looks wrong, stop and contact us first.
